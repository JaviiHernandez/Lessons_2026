📖 The Story — Casa Caos

Tell pupils this at the start: "Today we are going to visit a very messy house — Casa Caos! The lights are on all day, the fridge door is open, and the taps are running! What a waste! We need Eco Heroes to visit every room, find out what room it is, and learn how to fix it. Can your team save Casa Caos and make it an Eco House?"

⏱️ Suggested Timeline

0–5 min
Introduction
Tell the story of Casa Caos. Introduce the 5 rooms using the poster. Pre-teach key vocabulary on the board.
5–10 min
Team Setup
Form teams of 4–5. Hand out Team Boards. Assign roles: Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Decider.
10–35 min
Hunt! (5 × ~5 min)
Teams rotate through 5 room stations. Scan QR → read clues → type answer → collect badge. Encourage eco tip discussion at each station.
35–42 min
Debrief
Gather all teams. Ask: "What eco tips did you learn?" Discuss energy and water saving at home.
42–45 min
Awards
Hand out diplomas. Give medals to stand-out pupils in each role. Celebrate Eco Heroes!

🌿 Eco Vocabulary

Turn off the lights! Practice: mime switching the lights off.
Close the tap! Practice: mime turning a tap off.
Close the fridge! Practice: act out closing a fridge door.
Save water / Save energy! Practice: thumbs up + repeat together as a class.
It's daytime! Use sunlight! Practice: point to the window and the sky.
Unplug the charger! Practice: mime pulling a plug out of the wall.

♻️ Differentiation Tips

Support (Lower level)Allow pupils to use a word bank. Accept first letter only as hint. Read clues aloud as a class first.
Extension (Higher level)After each station, ask: "What is the problem in this room? What is the solution?" Write sentences.
EAL PupilsPair with a supportive peer. Allow L1 to discuss, then give English answer together.
Early FinishersAsk teams to draw their own "Eco Tip" poster for one room in English.

💬 Useful Classroom Language

"Scan the QR code, please!"Scanner: holds the device and scans at each station.
"Read the clues out loud!"Reader: reads all 4 clues aloud to the team.
"What do you think?"Decider asks team before typing the final answer.
"What is the eco tip?"Use after success — ask teams to summarise the tip in English.
