Teacher Guide — The Christmas Sports Hunt
Lesson 4: Christmas · Sports · English-Speaking Countries · Year 3 (Age 8–9)
⏱ 50 min 🗺️ 5 Stations 👥 Teams of 4–5 📚 Grammar + Vocab 🌍 Culture A1 ⚽🏈🏒🏄🐎
📖 The Story — Santa's Big Problem
"Listen carefully! Santa has a big problem this year! 🎅 His elves are very, very lazy. They watch TV all day, eat too many cookies, and don't move! Santa is worried: 'If my elves don't exercise, who will help me deliver all the presents on Christmas Eve?'

So Santa creates a special team: The Lazy Detectives! And YOU are the new detectives! Your mission: travel around the world to 5 English-speaking countries and discover their famous Christmas sport! Learn how kids stay strong and healthy at Christmas — and help Santa's lazy elves get fit again!

Find all 5 Christmas sports → become a Christmas Sports Champion! 🏆🎄 Are you ready? Let's hunt! ⭐"
💡 Read with energy — pause for effect at "lazy", act out eating cookies and watching TV. Build suspense before "Your mission". Ask: "Are you ready to be a Lazy Detective?" Class replies "YES!"
⏱ Suggested Timeline (50 min)
0–5 min
Warm-up: Christmas + Sports vocabulary brainstorm — mind map on the board. Ask: "What sports do you know?" Show flag emojis and ask which countries they are.
5–10 min
Story introduction: Read Santa's lazy elves story aloud dramatically. Display the poster. Assign teams and roles (Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Decider). Hand out team boards.
10–40 min
The Hunt: Teams rotate through the 5 stations. Scanner scans QR → Reader reads clues aloud → team discusses in English → Decider picks the answer → Writer records it. Collect gifts on team board. Character cards awarded on completion.
40–45 min
Sharing time: Each team says their favourite sport in English: "I like…" / "I can…" / "He is playing…" Ask: "What sport does Lucy play?" — pupils answer in Present Continuous.
45–50 min
Awards ceremony: Announce the Christmas Sports Champion team 🏆. Hand out role medals. Award diplomas. Optional: each pupil mimes a sport and class guesses: "Are you playing hockey?"

⚡ Grammar Spotlight — Three Key Structures
Present Continuous · Can/Can't · Like/Don't like
✅ Present Continuous
"I'm skating on ice."
"He is riding a horse."
❌ Present Continuous Neg.
"I'm not playing football."
"She isn't surfing today."
💙 Can / Can't
"I can score goals!"
"I can't skate fast."
⭐ Like / Don't like
"I like soccer!"
"I don't like cricket."
🌍 Cultural Note — Christmas Sports Around the World
Help pupils understand that Christmas looks different in different countries! In the UK, Boxing Day (26th Dec) is a huge football day — a tradition since the 1880s. In the USA, NFL Christmas Day games are watched by millions of families. In Canada, pond hockey on frozen lakes is a beloved Christmas tradition. In Australia and New Zealand, Christmas is in summer — families enjoy beach cricket and surfing! In Ireland, the Leopardstown Christmas horse racing festival dates back to 1888. Use this activity to build intercultural awareness and link language learning to real-world geography.

🎯 Differentiation Tips
💙 Support (SEN / Lower)
Provide visual flash cards with sport names and flag images. Pre-teach key vocabulary before the activity. Allow pupils to point to the answer rather than say it. Pair with a stronger reader.
🔥 Extension (Higher)
Challenge: "Write 3 sentences about your favourite sport using I'm + verb-ing, I can and I like." Can they name another Christmas tradition from any English-speaking country?
🌐 EAL Learners
Use bilingual support for clue reading. Show sport images alongside text. Allow home-language discussion before responding in English. Focus assessment on comprehension, not production accuracy.
⚡ Early Finishers
Challenge: "Design a new Christmas sport! Draw it and write 4 sentences: I'm + -ing, I can, I like, I don't like." Or: "Can you name the capital city of each country?"
