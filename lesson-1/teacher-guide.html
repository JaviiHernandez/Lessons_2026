Teacher Guide — Feelings for the Future
Lesson 1: Emotions & Hope for a Greener Planet (Feelings + Can/Can't) · Year 3 (Age 8–9)
⏱ 50 min 🌈 5 Feelings 👥 Teams of 4–5 📚 Can/Can't + Feelings 💚 Green Habits A1 😟😢😠😲😄
📖 The Story — Tim Needs Hope for the Future
"Listen carefully, heroes! 🌈 My name is Tim and I come from the future. In my world the Planet is not green: the air is dirty, the forests have no trees and the sea is full of plastic. The people are very worried and they have no hope.

I need the Year 3 heroes of today! As Hope Heroes, your mission: travel to 5 feelings, read the clues and find the right emotion.

Show the future what we can do — we can plant trees, recycle and save water; we can't throw plastic in the sea. Little by little, worried becomes happy…

Find all 5 feelings → give the future HOPE for a green, safe home! 🌈🏆 Are you ready? Let's go! 💚"
💡 Show emotion flashcards or make the faces yourself. Pause and mime each feeling (worried, sad, angry). Ask: "How do you feel?" Model: "I am happy." Then add the grammar: "We can recycle!"
⏱ Suggested Timeline (50 min)
0–5 min
Warm-up: Feelings faces. Show or mime emotion faces; the class names them (happy, sad, angry, worried, surprised). Play a quick "mirror" game. Model "I am happy / He is sad."
5–10 min
Story introduction: Read Tim's story aloud. Display the poster. Assign teams and roles (Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Green Keeper). Hand out team boards.
10–40 min
The Hunt: Teams rotate through the 5 feelings. Scanner scans the QR → Reader reads the clues → team discusses in English → Speaker picks the feeling → Writer records it and one green "We can…" action. Tick the team board. Fun fact cards awarded on completion.
40–45 min
Sharing time: Build the feelings journey on the board: worried → sad → angry → surprised → happy → hopeful. For each one, add a green action: "We can plant trees / recycle / save water."
45–50 min
Awards ceremony: Announce the Hope Hero team 🏆. Hand out role medals. Award diplomas. Optional: mime a feeling — the class guesses it and says one green action we can do!

"I can plant a tree."

"We can't waste water."
"We can't throw plastic in the sea."

"I am happy."
"She is sad. They are worried."

"Can you help? — Yes, I can!"
"How do you feel? — I feel hopeful."
🌍 Feelings Around the World & Green Habits
Feelings are the same all around the world — a happy face and a sad face look the same in every country! In English we describe a feeling with be: "I am happy", "she is sad". Practise naming the five feelings from the hunt:
😄 happy 😢 sad 😠 angry 😟 worried 😲 surprised
Then link feelings to action with can / can't: we can recycle, plant trees, save water and ride a bike; we can't waste energy or drop litter — small daily actions turn worry into hope for a greener Planet! 🌱
🎓 LOMLOE Curriculum Competencies (Year 3)
CCL — Linguistic
Pupils read the clues, name feelings, use can / can't and write the feeling with a green action on the team board.
CP — Plurilingual
Pupils compare feeling words in English and in their own language and see that emotions are universal.
STEM
Pupils link causes and effects — pollution, plastic and waste — and sequence the story from problem to solution.
CD — Digital
Pupils use tablets/phones safely to scan QR codes and interact with the digital riddle.
CPSAA — Personal & Social
Pupils recognise and name their own emotions, build empathy and talk about feelings (emotional literacy).
CC — Citizenship
Pupils learn sustainable habits — recycle, save water and energy, protect nature — for a greener future.

🎯 Differentiation Tips
💙 Support (SEN / Lower)
Provide emotion face cards and picture prompts. Pre-teach the feeling words. Allow pupils to point to the face or make the face rather than say it. Pair with a stronger reader.
🔥 Extension (Higher)
Challenge: "Write 3 sentences: I feel…, We can…, We can't…" Add one green action to each feeling and explain why it helps the Planet.
🌐 EAL Learners
Use emotion faces and pictures alongside the text. Allow home-language discussion before responding in English. Focus on comprehension, not production accuracy.
⚡ Early Finishers
Challenge: "Draw your own feelings face and a green action we CAN do." Or make a mini "We can / We can't" poster for the classroom wall.
