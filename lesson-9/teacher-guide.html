St. Patrick's Animal Hunt · A1 English · Year 3

⏱ Lesson Timeline (45–50 min)
0–5
min
🌟 Warm-up — The Legend
Show the poster. Read the legend aloud with expression. Ask: "Do you know Saint Patrick?" / "What animals do you know in English?" Introduce the 5 animals on the board with flashcards.
💡 Tip: Act out St. Patrick expelling snakes — kids love it!
5–10
min
👥 Set Up — Teams & Roles
Form teams of 4–5. Hand out team boards. Assign roles (Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Decider). Let teams choose a team name. Place QR cards at 5 stations around the room.
💡 Tip: Number desks/areas 1–5 with station colour dots so teams navigate easily.
10–40
min
🎮 Game — Animal Hunt (30 min)
Teams move freely between stations in any order. Each team scans QR → reads riddle → submits answer → gets card. Circulate to monitor, encourage, and help stuck teams. Give +1 hint token if a team is stuck after 3 minutes.
💡 Tip: Teams that finish early can help decode the legend or create their own riddle.
40–45
min
🏆 Closing — Celebration & Reflection
Award medals and diplomas to all teams. Do a quick whole-class review: show each animal, ask "What is it?", review clue vocabulary. Thumbs up/down exit ticket: "Did you enjoy it? Did you learn new words?"
💡 Tip: Take photos of teams with their completed boards!

Adaptations & Evaluation
🧠 ADHD & SEN Adaptations
✏️
Short Instructions
Max 1 line per step. Use the role cards as visual anchors. Repeat instructions one-to-one if needed.
🎨
Colour Coding
Each station has a unique colour. Use matching dot stickers on desks so students navigate independently.
⭐
Visible Progress
The team board's 5-slot grid gives constant visual feedback. Celebrate each card collected immediately.
🏃
Movement Built In
Teams physically move between stations. This channels energy productively and resets attention.
⏱️
Time Awareness
Each riddle shows "~5 min". Use a visual timer on the board. Give 2-min warnings before moving stations.
🔁
Rotating Roles
Rotate roles at each station. Everyone has a defined job — reduces social anxiety and off-task behaviour.
🎯
Clear Goal Always Visible
"5 animals = Victory!" on every screen and card. Students always know what they are working towards.
💡
Scaffolded Hints
The riddle app gives an extra letter hint after 2 wrong attempts automatically — no teacher intervention needed.

🌟 Bonus Points System
🍀 Shamrock Stickers
Give 1 sticker per animal card collected. Students paste them in their notebook. 5 stickers = full star in class register.
⭐ +1 Grade Point
Teams completing all 5 stations earn +1 bonus point on the unit grade. Partial completion = participation mark.
