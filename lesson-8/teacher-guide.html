Teacher Guide — My Dream Job
Lesson 8: My Dream Job (Occupations) · Year 3 (Age 8–9)
⏱ 50 min 🗺️ 5 Children 👥 Teams of 4–5 📚 Grammar + Vocab ✊ Equality & Rights A1 🧵🩺🏺🌾✈️
📖 The Story — Helping Rural Schools in India
"Listen carefully, helpers! 🌟 In a small village in India, a new school opens its doors. Every child — girl and boy — has the right to learn.

Five children have big dreams! Agna, Kaito, Mara, Riley and Kanan each want a special dream job — but we don't know what they are yet!

As Dream Helpers, your mission: travel to 5 spots, read each child's clues, and discover their dream job. Show the world that with school, every child can follow their dream, because everyone is equal!

Discover all 5 dreams → become a Dream Helper! 🌟🏆 Are you ready, helpers? Let's discover their dreams! 📚"
💡 Read with warmth and wonder. Pause at "big dreams" and "everyone is equal". Locate India (and Canada, Indi's home in Lesson 7) on a world map. Ask: "What is YOUR dream job?" Class shares: "I want to be a…"
⏱ Suggested Timeline (50 min)
0–5 min
Warm-up: Jobs brainstorm — mind map on the board. Ask: "What jobs do you know?" "What do you want to be?" Show job emoji flashcards and model "I want to be a…".
5–10 min
Story introduction: Read the India village-school story aloud. Display the poster. Assign teams and roles (Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Explorer). Hand out team boards.
10–40 min
The Hunt: Teams rotate through the 5 spots. Scanner scans QR → Reader reads the child's clues → team discusses in English → Explorer picks the job → Writer records it. Tick the team board. Fun fact cards awarded on completion.
40–45 min
Sharing time: Each team shares: "Agna wants to be a weaver." / "I want to be…" / "I don't want to be…" Discuss: can girls and boys do any job? (Yes — equality!)
45–50 min
Awards ceremony: Announce the Dream Helper team 🏆. Hand out role medals. Award diplomas. Optional: each pupil mimes a job — class guesses: "Do you want to be a pilot?"

✊ Equality, Rights & Cross-Cutting Themes
Use this lesson to weave in values alongside language learning. Every child has the right to an education — but in some places inequality means not everyone can go to school. Discuss that girls and boys are equal and can do any job: a girl can be a pilot, a boy can be a weaver. Celebrate diversity of jobs and cultures, and the idea that we should care for others and help save every child's chance to learn. India is a great window for intercultural awareness — handlooms, diyas, farming and festivals — shown with respect and curiosity.
🎓 LOMLOE Curriculum Competencies (Year 3)
CCL — Linguistic
Pupils read clues, listen to teammates, speak in short English sentences, and write the job on the team board.
CP — Plurilingual
Pupils meet children from India and compare jobs and cultures across languages and countries.
STEM
Pupils reason about how each job works (a wheel spins, a plane flies) and follow logical clues.
CD — Digital
Pupils use tablets/phones safely to scan QR codes and interact with the digital riddle.
CPSAA — Personal & Social
Pupils take turns, listen, share roles, and reflect on their own dreams and on caring for others.
CC — Citizenship
Pupils learn about the right to education, equality and diversity — linking language to global values.

🎯 Differentiation Tips
💙 Support (SEN / Lower)
Provide visual flashcards with job images and word labels. Pre-teach key vocabulary. Allow pupils to point to the answer rather than say it. Pair with a stronger reader.
🔥 Extension (Higher)
Challenge: "Write 4 sentences: I want to be…, I don't want to be…, A … helps/makes…, and why." Can they explain why every child has the right to learn?
🌐 EAL Learners
Use bilingual support for clue reading. Show job images alongside text. Allow home-language discussion before responding in English. Focus assessment on comprehension, not production accuracy.
⚡ Early Finishers
Challenge: "Draw your dream job and write 5 sentences: I want to be…, A … (verb)…, I like…, I don't want to be…" Or design a poster: "Girls and boys can do any job!"
