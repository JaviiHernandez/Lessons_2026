📖 Every Child Has the Right to Dream!
In a small village in India, a new school opens its doors. 🏫 Every child — girl and boy — has the right to learn.

Five children have big dreams! Agna, Kaito, Mara, Riley and Kanan each have a special dream job.

As Dream Helpers, your mission is to travel to 5 spots, solve a riddle at each one, and discover each child's dream job — showing the world that with school, EVERY child can follow their dream, because everyone is equal! 🌟📚
