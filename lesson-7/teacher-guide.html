Teacher Guide — Non-teachy Hobbies Hunt
Lesson 7: I Have Got a Teachy Friend (Hobbies) · Year 3 (Age 8–9)
⏱ 50 min 🗺️ 5 Spots 👥 Teams of 4–5 📚 Grammar + Vocab 🌳 Screen-Free Habits A1 🎸⛺🥄📚🌱
📖 The Story — Tim & Indi's Screen-Free Mission
"Listen carefully, scouts! 🧭 My friend Tim and his Canadian friend Indi sit in front of screens all day long, like hypnotised! They are bored and tired, and they never go outdoors.

Five amazing screen-free hobbies have been forgotten! Tim and Indi need YOU to teach the children of the future how to have fun without technology.

As Hobby Scouts, your mission: travel to 5 secret spots, solve the riddles, discover the non-teachy hobbies, and earn a scout badge at each spot!

Discover all 5 hobbies → become a Non-teachy Hobbies Scout! 🏅🏆 Are you ready, scouts? Let's unplug and explore! 🌳"
💡 Read with energy — pause for effect at "hypnotised" and "forgotten". Mime looking at a phone, then put it down. Build suspense before "Your mission". Ask: "Are you ready, scouts?" Class replies "YES!"
⏱ Suggested Timeline (50 min)
0–5 min
Warm-up: Hobby brainstorm — mind map on the board. Ask: "What hobbies do you like?" "Which hobbies need a screen (teachy) and which don't (non-teachy)?" Show hobby emoji flashcards.
5–10 min
Story introduction: Read the story about Tim & Indi aloud dramatically. Display the poster. Assign teams and roles (Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Explorer). Hand out team boards.
10–40 min
The Hunt: Teams rotate through the 5 spots. Scanner scans QR → Reader reads clues aloud → team discusses in English → Explorer picks the hobby → Writer records it. Tick the team board. Fun fact cards awarded on completion.
40–45 min
Sharing time: Each team says their favourite hobby: "I like…" / "I don't like…" / "Yesterday I played…" Build a "Screen-Free Weekend" plan together on the board: indoor + outdoor hobbies!
45–50 min
Awards ceremony: Announce the Non-teachy Hobbies Scout team 🏆. Hand out role medals. Award diplomas. Optional: each pupil mimes a hobby — class guesses: "Do you play the guitar?"

🌳 Screen-Free & Cross-Cutting Themes
Use this lesson to weave in healthy habits alongside language learning. The teachy vs non-teachy challenge asks pupils to sort hobbies: do they need a screen, or not? Encourage them to unplug, switch off their devices and reduce screen time. Highlight indoor hobbies (cooking, reading) and outdoor ones (camping, gardening). Tim's friend Indi is from Canada — a great chance to find the country on a map and talk about outdoor life. Build awareness that real-world hobbies keep us healthy, happy and connected.
🎓 LOMLOE Curriculum Competencies (Year 3)
CCL — Linguistic
Pupils read clues, listen to teammates, speak in short English sentences, and write the answer on the team board.
CP — Plurilingual
Pupils compare hobby words across languages and meet Indi from Canada — a window onto other cultures.
STEM
Pupils follow step-by-step instructions (grow a plant, cook, write a song) and reason about cause and effect.
CD — Digital
Pupils use tablets/phones safely to scan QR codes — and reflect on healthy, balanced screen time.
CPSAA — Personal & Social
Pupils take turns, listen, share roles, and reflect on healthy habits and unplugging from screens.
CC — Citizenship
Pupils value outdoor life, nature and free time — linking language to a healthy, balanced lifestyle.

🎯 Differentiation Tips
💙 Support (SEN / Lower)
Provide visual flashcards with hobby images and word labels. Pre-teach key vocabulary. Allow pupils to point to the answer rather than say it. Pair with a stronger reader.
🔥 Extension (Higher)
Challenge: "Write 4 sentences about your hobbies using I play, Yesterday I played, I like, and I don't like." Can they sort 8 hobbies into teachy vs non-teachy?
🌐 EAL Learners
Use bilingual support for clue reading. Show hobby images alongside text. Allow home-language discussion before responding in English. Focus assessment on comprehension, not production accuracy.
⚡ Early Finishers
Challenge: "Design a screen-free weekend! Draw it and write 5 sentences: I play…, Yesterday I…, I like…, I don't like…, indoor/outdoor." Or: "Invent a new non-teachy hobby!"

My Hobby poster — Pupils draw their favourite non-teachy hobby and write 3 sentences in English.
Screen-time diary — One week diary: "Yesterday I switched off the screen and I…"
Hobby interview — In pairs: "What hobbies do you like? What did you do yesterday?"
Teachy or non-teachy? — Sorting game with hobby flashcards: 2 columns on the board.
