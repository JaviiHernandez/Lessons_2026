Yoghurt Cake
Station 1 · Easy & Yummy
📝 What you need
1 cup of yoghurt
3 eggs
2 cups of flour
200 ml of milk
2 spoons of sugar
a pinch of salt
👨‍🍳 How to make it
1Mix yoghurt + sugar in a bowl.
2Add eggs and milk. Stir!
3Add flour. Mix again.
4Bake 30 minutes in the oven! 🔥

Vegetable Soup
Station 3 · Warm & Healthy
📝 What you need
3 carrots 🥕
1 onion
2 potatoes
1 litre of water
a pinch of salt
1 spoon of oil
👨‍🍳 How to make it

2Add 1 litre of water in a pot.

4Cook 20 minutes. Yummy! 😋
