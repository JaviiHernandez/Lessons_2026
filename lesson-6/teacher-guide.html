Teacher Guide — Delicious Food Detectives
Lesson 6: Food · Healthy Habits · Year 3 (Age 8–9)
⏱ 50 min 🗺️ 5 Kitchens 👥 Teams of 4–5 📚 Grammar + Vocab 🌍 Healthy Habits A1 🥛🍗🥕🍪🐟
📖 The Story — Year 2026: The Lost Ingredients
"Listen carefully, detectives! 🔍 In the year 2026, children have forgotten how to eat healthy food! They only eat junk food, they waste food, they don't drink water, and they're not happy.

Five magical ingredients have disappeared from the World Kitchen! Professor Yum 👨‍🔬 — a friendly food scientist — has called YOU for help!

As Food Detectives, your mission: travel to 5 secret kitchens, solve the riddles, find the lost ingredients, and bring back the secrets of healthy eating — so the children of the future can be healthy and happy again!

Find all 5 ingredients → become a Healthy Food Detective! 🥗🏆 Are you ready, detectives? Let's investigate! 🔍"
💡 Read with energy — pause for effect at "disappeared" and "Year 2026". Pretend to put on a magnifying glass. Build suspense before "Your mission". Ask: "Are you ready, food detectives?" Class replies "YES!"
⏱ Suggested Timeline (50 min)
0–5 min
Warm-up: Food vocabulary brainstorm — mind map on the board. Ask: "What healthy food do you like?" "What food is not healthy?" Show food emoji flashcards.
5–10 min
Story introduction: Read the Year 2026 story aloud dramatically. Display the poster. Assign teams and roles (Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Decider). Hand out team boards.
10–40 min
The Hunt: Teams rotate through the 5 kitchens. Scanner scans QR → Reader reads clues aloud → team discusses in English → Decider picks the answer → Writer records it. Collect ingredients on team board. Character cards awarded on completion.
40–45 min
Sharing time: Each team says their favourite ingredient: "I like…" / "I don't like…" / "I'm eating…" Build a balanced plate together on the board: protein + vegetables + grains + fruit + water!
45–50 min
Awards ceremony: Announce the Healthy Food Detective team 🏆. Hand out role medals. Award diplomas. Optional: each pupil mimes eating an ingredient — class guesses: "Are you eating yoghurt?"

🌍 Healthy Habits & Cross-Cutting Themes
Use this lesson to weave in healthy habits alongside language learning. Highlight that balanced plates mix protein + vegetables + grains + fruit, and a glass of water instead of sugary drinks. In Mediterranean countries, families eat fresh vegetables, fish, and olive oil — like in our story! Around the world, many children don't have enough food: we are lucky, so let's never waste what we eat. Drinking water, eating fruit, and moving the body every day are simple habits that build a healthy life. Use this activity to build healthy lifestyle awareness and link language to the real world.
🎓 LOMLOE Curriculum Competencies (Year 3)
CCL — Linguistic
Pupils read clues, listen to teammates, speak in short English sentences, and write the answer on the team board.
CP — Plurilingual
Pupils compare food vocabulary across languages and learn English-specific words (junk, sour, crunchy).
STEM
Pupils use measurement language (a cup of, two spoons of) and basic counting in real food contexts.
CD — Digital
Pupils use tablets/phones safely to scan QR codes and interact with the digital riddle.
CPSAA — Personal & Social
Pupils take turns, listen, share roles, and reflect on healthy habits and food waste.
CC — Citizenship
Pupils learn about world hunger, food waste, and balanced eating — tying language to global awareness.

🎯 Differentiation Tips
💙 Support (SEN / Lower)
Provide visual flashcards with food images and adjective labels. Pre-teach key vocabulary. Allow pupils to point to the answer rather than say it. Pair with a stronger reader.
🔥 Extension (Higher)
Challenge: "Write 4 sentences about your favourite food using I'm, I like, I don't like, and a quantifier." Can they design a balanced plate and label all 4 parts in English?
🌐 EAL Learners
Use bilingual support for clue reading. Show food images alongside text. Allow home-language discussion before responding in English. Focus assessment on comprehension, not production accuracy.
⚡ Early Finishers
Challenge: "Design a new healthy meal! Draw it and write 5 sentences: I'm + -ing, I like, I don't like, a cup of…, two spoons of…" Or: "What food is wasted at home? How can we save it?"

🌱 Follow-up Activities
My Balanced Plate poster — Pupils draw their lunch on a paper plate and label each food group in English.
Food waste log — One week diary: "Today I didn't waste my…"
Recipe time — Pupils write a simple recipe using "a cup of / a spoon of" — read aloud in pairs.
Healthy or junk? — Sorting game with food flashcards: 2 columns on the board.
