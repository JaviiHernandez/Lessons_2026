📖 Help Professor Yum Save Healthy Eating!
In the Year 2026, children have forgotten how to eat healthy food! 🚨 They only eat junk food, they waste food, they don't drink water, and they're not happy.

Five magical ingredients have disappeared from the World Kitchen! 🌍 Professor Yum 👨‍🔬, a friendly food scientist, has called YOU for help.

As Food Detectives, you must travel to 5 secret kitchens, solve a riddle at each one, find the lost ingredient, and bring back the secrets of healthy eating — so the children of the future can be healthy and happy again! 🥗💚
