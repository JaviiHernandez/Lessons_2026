Teacher Guide — Routines Around the World
Lesson 11: Habits for a Greener Future (Routines + Time) · Year 3 (Age 8–9)
⏱ 50 min 🕐 5 Times of day 👥 Teams of 4–5 📚 Grammar + Vocab 💚 Green Habits A1 ⏰🥣🏫🚿🛌
📖 The Story — The Children of the Future Forgot the Clock
"Listen carefully, heroes! ⏰ In the future, children have forgotten the hours of the clock! They are always late, their heads are in a muddle, and they don't know what a routine is!

They need the Year 3 students of today to help them. As Routine Heroes, your mission: travel to 5 times of the day, read each clock, solve the riddles, and discover the perfect daily routine.

And don't forget to teach them green habits — turn off the lights, save water and unplug — for a healthier, greener Planet!

Find all 5 routines → become a Routine Hero! ⏰🏆 Are you ready? Let's begin! 🌍"
💡 Bring a toy clock or draw clock faces on the board. Pause at "always late" and "muddle". Mime being confused, then look at a clock with relief. Ask: "What time do you wake up?" Class answers: "At 7 o'clock!"
⏱ Suggested Timeline (50 min)
0–5 min
Warm-up: Clock & routine brainstorm. Show clock faces and ask "What time is it?" Mind-map daily routines on the board (wake up, breakfast, school…). Model "I wake up at 7 o'clock."
5–10 min
Story introduction: Read the "future children" story aloud. Display the poster. Assign teams and roles (Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Timekeeper). Hand out team boards.
10–40 min
The Hunt: Teams rotate through the 5 times. Scanner scans QR → Reader reads the time and clues → team discusses in English → Speaker picks the routine → Writer records it. Tick the team board. Fun fact cards awarded on completion.
40–45 min
Sharing time: Build the perfect day on the board in order: "At 7 o'clock I wake up. At half past seven I have breakfast…" Add green habits: turn off the lights, save water, unplug!
45–50 min
Awards ceremony: Announce the Routine Hero team 🏆. Hand out role medals. Award diplomas. Optional: mime a routine — class guesses the time and the action: "It's 9 o'clock at night — you go to bed!"

🌍 Time Zones Around the World & Green Habits
The Earth turns, so the same routine happens at a different time around the world. When children wake up in Spain, others are sleeping or already at school. Use a globe to explore these time zones:
🇬🇧 UK same / 1h behind 🇺🇸 USA night 🇨🇦 Canada night 🇦🇺 Australia evening 🇳🇿 New Zealand evening
Weave in green habits all day: turn off the lights, save water, take short showers, unplug devices and reduce waste — small daily actions for a greener Planet! 🌱

STEM
Pupils read analogue and digital clocks (o'clock, half past, quarter to) and sequence the day.
CD — Digital
Pupils use tablets/phones safely to scan QR codes and interact with the digital riddle.
CPSAA — Personal & Social
Pupils reflect on healthy daily routines, sleep, hygiene and time management.
CC — Citizenship
Pupils learn sustainable habits — save water and energy, reduce, reuse — for a greener future.

🎯 Differentiation Tips
💙 Support (SEN / Lower)
Provide clock flashcards and routine pictures. Pre-teach key verbs. Allow pupils to point to the routine and the clock rather than say it. Pair with a stronger reader.
🔥 Extension (Higher)
Challenge: "Write your own daily routine with 5 times: At 7 o'clock I…, In the morning I…, At night I…" Add one green habit to each.
🌐 EAL Learners
Use clock visuals and routine images alongside text. Allow home-language discussion before responding in English. Focus on comprehension, not production accuracy.
⚡ Early Finishers
Challenge: "Design a poster: My Green Day! Draw 5 routines with the time and a green habit for each." Or compare the time in two countries.

🌱 Follow-up Activities

Around the world — Find a country on the map and guess: morning, evening or night?
