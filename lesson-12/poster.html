📖 The Beach is Full of Rubbish!
Tim and the Year 3 heroes are on holiday at the beach! 🏖️ But oh no — the sand and the sea are full of rubbish: cans, bottles and plastic bags. 🗑️

As Beach Guardians, your mission: travel to 5 stations, read the clues and find the beach word.

Teach the future the 3 Rs — Reduce, Reuse, Recycle — so turtles, jellyfish and fish can live in a clean, safe ocean! 🌊💙
