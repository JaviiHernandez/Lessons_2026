Teacher Guide — Beach Guardians
Lesson 12: Recycling & Ocean Pollution (Beach Vocabulary + Present Simple) · Year 3 (Age 8–9)
⏱ 50 min 🏖️ 5 Stations 👥 Teams of 4–5 📚 Present Simple ♻️ The 3 Rs A1 🏰🩱🧴🐢💜
📖 The Story — The Beach is Full of Rubbish
"Listen carefully, Guardians! 🏖️ We are on holiday at the beach with Tim. But look — the sand and the sea are full of rubbish: cans, bottles and plastic bags! The sea animals are in danger.

As Beach Guardians, your mission: travel to 5 stations, read the clues and find the beach word.

Teach the people of the future the 3 Rs — Reduce, Reuse, Recycle — so turtles, jellyfish and fish can live in a clean, safe ocean!

Find all 5 words → become a Beach Guardian! 🏆 Work together — every role helps! Are you ready? Let's go! 🌊"
💡 Bring a few clean "beach" items (a bucket, a sunscreen bottle, a plastic bottle) and a recycling bin. Mime picking up litter on the sand. Ask: "What can we reduce, reuse and recycle?" Model: "We recycle the bottle."
⏱ Suggested Timeline (50 min)
0–5 min
Warm-up: Beach brainstorm. Show beach pictures and items; elicit beach words. Introduce the three recycling bins (plastic, paper, glass). Model "We recycle the bottle."
5–10 min
Story introduction: Read Tim's story. Display the poster. Assign teams and roles (Reader, Writer, Scanner, Speaker, Recycler). Stress positive interdependence: the team only wins if everyone does their job. Hand out team boards.
10–40 min
The Hunt: Teams rotate through the 5 stations. Scanner scans the QR → Reader reads the clues → team discusses in English → Speaker says the word → Writer records it and a 3R action → Recycler names Reduce / Reuse / Recycle. Fun fact cards awarded on completion.
40–45 min
Sharing time: Build a clean beach on the board. Sort rubbish into Reduce / Reuse / Recycle. Practise present simple: "The turtle swims. We recycle the cans. We keep the sea clean."
45–50 min
Awards ceremony: Announce the Beach Guardian team 🏆. Hand out role medals. Award diplomas. Optional: sort real or picture "rubbish" into the three bins as a team.

🌊 The 3 Rs & Ocean Pollution
Our oceans are full of plastic. It hurts sea animals — turtles eat plastic bags, and fish get sick. The best way to help is the 3 Rs:
🗑️ Reduce 🔁 Reuse ♻️ Recycle 🧤 Pick up litter 🐠 Reef-safe
Small actions on the beach — take your rubbish home, use a cloth bag, recycle bottles and cans — keep the ocean clean and safe for every sea animal! 🐢🐟🌊
🎓 LOMLOE Curriculum Competencies (Year 3)
CCL — Linguistic
Pupils read the clues, name beach words, use the present simple, and write sentences on the team board.
CP — Plurilingual
Pupils compare beach and recycling words in English and in their own language.
STEM
Pupils classify waste (plastic, paper, glass) and explore how pollution affects sea animals.
CD — Digital
Pupils use tablets/phones safely to scan QR codes and interact with the digital riddle.
CPSAA — Personal & Social
Pupils practise teamwork and positive interdependence — each role is needed for the team to win.
CC — Citizenship
Pupils learn sustainable habits — Reduce, Reuse, Recycle — to protect beaches and oceans.

🎯 Differentiation Tips
💙 Support (SEN / Lower)
Provide picture cards of the beach words and the three bins. Pre-teach key verbs. Allow pupils to point to the picture rather than say it. Pair with a stronger reader.
🔥 Extension (Higher)
Challenge: "Write 3 present-simple sentences: I …, The turtle …, We recycle …" Add why each 3R action helps the ocean.
🌐 EAL Learners
Use pictures of beach items and sea animals alongside the text. Allow home-language discussion before responding in English. Focus on comprehension.
⚡ Early Finishers
Challenge: "Draw a clean beach and label it: We recycle. We reduce. We reuse." Or design a poster to save the sea turtles.
